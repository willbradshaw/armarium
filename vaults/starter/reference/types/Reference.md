Pages with `type: "[[Reference]]"` are structural, taxonomic, or index pages, such as campaign overviews and language catalogues.

## Schema

A typed Reference note’s frontmatter requires only `type: "[[Reference]]"`.
Custom fields are allowed, and the body may be empty or contain free Markdown.
Untyped indexes, type pages and status definitions (`applies_to`) are supporting
documents and do not need this type.

See the [Reference schema](../schemas/reference.schema.json).
