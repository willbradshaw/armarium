# The Third Bell

[[Orlan Countinghouse]] wants to sell [[Glass Petrel]] to recover an alleged unpaid repair advance. The [[Glass Petrel Crew]] disputes the charge. [[Captain Mara Vey]] offers a way to contest the sale; the [[Shoal Chart]] she entrusts to the party carries marks she will not explain.

Play centers on daring escapes, public promises and loyalties tested under pressure. The setting has sail, steel, black-powder signals and plenty of room for impossible luck; no particular rules system is assumed.
