# The Third Bell

The Glass Petrel is one hearing away from being sold to settle a debt its crew disputes. A pilot offers help, a countinghouse wants silence, and a shoal chart carries marks nobody will explain.

Play centers on daring escapes, public promises and loyalties tested under pressure. The setting has sail, steel, black-powder signals and plenty of room for impossible luck; no particular rules system is assumed.
