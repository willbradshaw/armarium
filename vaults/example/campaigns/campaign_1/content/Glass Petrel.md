## Notes

The cutter has patched ochre sails, a shallow draft and a galley stove that draws
poorly in an easterly wind. A dry document chest is bolted beneath the companionway.
Its light hull favors shoal passages over a straight race against a larger ship.

[[Orlan Countinghouse]] attempted to sell the vessel against a disputed debt in
[[S-1-001]]. [[Esme Calder]] secured a hearing, and the [[Glass Petrel Crew]] retained
possession. In [[S-1-002]], [[Glass Petrel Crew|the party]] sailed it out of [[Port Briselle]] using the
[[Brass Harbor Seal]] to authenticate its passage.
