# Prep for the hearing

Loose preparation for the public hearing that [[Orlan Countinghouse]] must
grant under [[The Bell Accord]] before it can sell [[Glass Petrel]].

- Who speaks for the crew? [[Esme Calder]] wants to; [[Talia Venn]] would
  rather the ship simply left.
- Possible complications: the countinghouse produces the repair ledger; a
  rival captain bids for the cutter at the hearing.
- If the crew runs instead, [[Quay Nine]] is the only berth they can leave
  from unseen.
