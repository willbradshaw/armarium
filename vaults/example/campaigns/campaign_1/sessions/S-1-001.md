# Notes

## Preamble

[[Rowan]] and [[Ellis]] attended. The GM cut [[C-1-0002]] during preparation.

## Events

- [[Orlan Countinghouse]]'s officer posted a notice proposing to sell [[Glass Petrel]] against an alleged unpaid repair advance.
- [[Talia Venn]] crossed the rooftop market in [[Port Briselle]] and reached [[Quay Nine]] before the auction officer.
- [[Esme Calder]] rang the bell and invoked [[The Bell Accord]], delaying [[Glass Petrel]]'s sale until a hearing before the [[Free Pilots Assembly]].
- [[Captain Mara Vey]] explained that the stamped [[Brass Harbor Seal]] permitted a crossing at the third bell; [[C-1-0001]] was revealed.
- [[Talia Venn]] obtained the seal and passage papers. [[Glass Petrel Crew|The party]] boarded [[Glass Petrel]], where [[Esme Calder]] took [[Blue Signal Flare]] from its stores for a departure signal.
- The [[Glass Petrel Crew]] agreed to contest the repair charge and seek certification from the [[Free Pilots Assembly]].
