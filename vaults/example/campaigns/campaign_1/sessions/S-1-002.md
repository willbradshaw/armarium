## Events

- [[Orlan Countinghouse]]'s messenger objected that the repair advance remained unpaid and demanded the auction proceed.

- [[Captain Mara Vey]] handed [[Shoal Chart]] to the [[Glass Petrel Crew]]. The company accepted collective custody and chose to depart together.
- [[Glass Petrel Crew|The crew]] saw two pencilled tide marks on the chart. [[Captain Mara Vey|Mara]] declined to explain them, giving a partial hint toward [[C-1-0003]].
- At [[Quay Nine]], [[Talia Venn]] authenticated the order with [[Brass Harbor Seal]] while [[Esme Calder|Esme]] fired [[Blue Signal Flare]]. The charge was consumed.
- [[Glass Petrel Crew|The crew]] sailed [[Glass Petrel]] out of [[Port Briselle]] before [[Orlan Countinghouse]] could demand another hearing.
