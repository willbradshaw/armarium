## The notice

- [GM] An officer of [[Orlan Countinghouse]] posts a sale notice on [[Glass Petrel]]. The notice claims an unpaid repair advance.
- [Esme] We dispute that charge. I want the contract produced at a public hearing.
- [Talia] Then I will reach the customs booth before the auction order is sealed.

## Across the roofs

- [GM] The clerk is taking the auction order down to [[Quay Nine]]. The awning below you hangs from a cargo hoist.
- [Talia] I hitch the loose line around the chimney and lower myself onto the awning. I want to reach the booth before the officer.
- [GM] You reach the booth as the clerk starts sorting the passage papers.

## Under the public bell

- [Esme] I ring the bell. Under [[The Bell Accord]], the sale of [[Glass Petrel]] must wait for a hearing.
- [GM] The officer demands a pilot witness before the petition can be certified.
- [Esme] Then we will take the petition to the [[Free Pilots Assembly]].
- [GM] [[Captain Mara Vey]] turns the passage order toward you. “Present this with the [[Brass Harbor Seal]] at the third bell, and the watch can open the boom.”
- [Talia] I collect the seal and the signed order. Esme can argue about the debt while I make sure we can actually leave.

## Back aboard

- [GM] Back aboard [[Glass Petrel]], the stores include a waxed signal charge.
- [Esme] I take the [[Blue Signal Flare]]. When the boom opens, one signal should spare us a great deal of shouting.
- [Talia] I check the running rigging. If we get our hearing, I want to be ready to cast off.
