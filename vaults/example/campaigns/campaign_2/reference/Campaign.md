# Before the Breakers

A swashbuckling salvage campaign following the [[Tern Salvage Company]] aboard
[[Tern]] on [[The Crownless Coast]]. Out of [[Port Briselle]], a pilot and a diver
race a storm to recover [[Copper Finch]] from [[The Red Teeth]], facing a rival
claim and a rescue that cannot wait for an agreement.

Daring seamanship, contested promises and solidarity under pressure shape play.
The opening is independent of The Third Bell's dispute; neither campaign assumes
an outcome from the other's unplayed sessions.

Working notes: [[Storm timing]] and the shared [[Coast design notes]].
