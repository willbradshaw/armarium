## Scene notes

- Reveal [[C-2-0001]] when the diver follows the tapping to the cabin.
- Hint at [[C-2-0002]] with the folded claim form. Its missing signature is only
  established if the party examines it; Silas can instead negotiate openly.
- Cut [[C-2-0004]] before play. The rescue and competing claims provide enough
  pressure without sabotage; do not treat the discarded explanation as fact.

## Encounters

Hold the sloop clear of the rocks while a diver frees a trapped captain.
[[Silas Rook]] can obstruct the approach from his rowing skiff, but a firm appeal
to rescue first may avoid a fight.

## Prepared rewards

A rescued captain who can negotiate payment for later salvage. No automatic
ownership of [[Copper Finch]] or her cargo follows from the rescue.

## Events

- In [[Port Briselle]], the [[Free Pilots Assembly]] asked the [[Tern Salvage Company]]
  to answer distress blows from [[Copper Finch]]. [[Darian Holt]] accepted; the
  company provisioned its own sloop, [[Tern]], and departed.
- At [[The Red Teeth]], [[Silas Rook]] approached in his skiff and asserted a prior
  salvage claim. He showed a seal on a folded form, but kept the lower section
  turned inward. This hinted at [[C-2-0002]] without revealing the missing signature.
- [[Darian Holt]] insisted that the rescue came first. Silas stood aside and agreed
  to discuss his claim afterward. Darian held [[Tern]] in the lee of the reef.
- [[Bram Kest]] followed tapping to the stern hatch of [[Copper Finch]]. He found
  [[Oren Vale]] alive beneath a fallen beam, revealing [[C-2-0001]], and freed him.
- Bram brought Oren aboard [[Tern]]. Oren said the rudder had broken and that he
  had sent his deckhands ashore before returning below. He asked to recover his
  breath before bargaining over the boat.
- The [[Tern Salvage Company]] deferred its salvage bargain. [[Copper Finch]] stayed
  on the reef in Oren's possession; [[Tern]] remained the company's vessel.
  The session ended beside the wreck, before any cargo recovery or return to port.
