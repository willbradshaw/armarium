## Scene notes

- Use [[C-2-0002]] if the company inspects the complete form or asks the
  [[Free Pilots Assembly]] to examine it. The form's requirements do not establish
  a general law governing all salvage on the coast.
- Offer [[C-2-0003]] if the party surveys the hold. Waiting for low water also
  brings the storm closer; the party can decide the cargo is not worth the risk.
- Give [[Darian Holt]] a choice between a sheltered approach that takes longer and
  an exposed route that preserves daylight. Give [[Bram Kest]] time to assess
  shifting timbers, with the cost of another inspection made clear. Let their
  appetite for pressing on and insistence on checking the wreck shape the decision.
- Any salvage payment requires Oren's agreement. Record an actual transfer only
  after play; neither the proposed inspection nor a return to port is an appearance.
