## Notes

Darian learned the approaches to [[Port Briselle]] ferrying crews between anchored
ships. He handles navigation, weather watching and supplies aboard [[Tern]]. He
likes teaching a nervous passenger to steer and remembers people by the voyages
he shared with them. His ambition is to chart sheltered landing places where
small crews can wait out a storm without paying for a harbor berth.

He talks easily, gives practical instructions and assumes that a workable route
can always be found. Calling off a passage feels like letting everyone down; he
sometimes needs [[Bram Kest]] to name the danger plainly before he will turn back.
He and Bram founded the [[Tern Salvage Company]] with equal shares. Darian keeps
the boat steady and the crew moving while Bram assesses the wreck.
