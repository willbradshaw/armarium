## Notes

[[Oren Vale]] owns and captains the boat. Her cargo is copper cooking pots bound
for [[Port Briselle]]. A broken rudder left her on the reef; in [[S-2-001]], the
rescuers found her stern hatch above water and her forward hold flooded.

Oren retained the boat after his rescue. [[Silas Rook]] asserted a salvage claim,
but no transfer or salvage agreement was concluded.
