## Notes

At low water the reef crowns resemble teeth above white surf. Between them lie
three narrow channels, two usable by shallow-draft cutters and one deep enough for
a laden merchantman at the right tide. A lookout can hear the outer breakers long
before seeing land in fog.

The [[Free Pilots Assembly]] maintains the channel knowledge for vessels
leaving [[Port Briselle]]. The reefs lie beyond the harbor authority's
markers; pilots treat promises made there as personal obligations.
