## Notes

Mara captains the pilot cutter [[Wake-lark]] and sits in the [[Free Pilots Assembly]]. A burn scar runs across her left palm; she wears a ribbon over it when negotiating. She asks opponents to name their terms before drawing her narrow sabre.

She knows [[The Red Teeth]] by the sound of surf against the sea wall. Her practical loyalties are to pilots whose livelihoods depend on access to [[Port Briselle]].
