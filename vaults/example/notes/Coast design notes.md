# Coast design notes

Working notes on [[The Crownless Coast]] that are not yet established in play.

- Keep the countinghouses of [[Port Briselle]] at odds with one another, so no
  single patron can settle a dispute alone.
- [[The Red Teeth]] should stay dangerous in every campaign: every crossing
  costs something.
- Open questions: who rings the harbor bell when the [[Free Pilots Assembly]]
  is divided? Does [[The Bell Accord]] bind ships from outside the Coast?
