Pages with `type: "[[types/Session]]"` are play-session records containing preparation and actual play notes, including events, interactions, and rewards.

Use [[templates/Session]] for the page structure.

Store records under `campaigns/campaign_1/sessions/`, named `S-1-001.md`,
`S-1-002.md`, etc. Set `session_number` and the campaign link. For another campaign,
update the path, campaign link and campaign number in the filename.

Select records in the ordered `prepared_clues`, `prepared_locations` and
`prepared_npcs` link lists (use `[]` for none). Preparation tables show live
canonical Clue `text` and Content `summary`, in selection order.
Enable Bases and the Frontmatter Markdown Links community plugin; see
[[reference/views/README|view instructions]] for setup and carry-forward.
Put session-specific instructions in Scene notes; record actual play under Events.
