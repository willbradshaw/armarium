# Magic Item Values

If characters can buy and sell magic items in a campaign, an item's rarity sets
its value in gold pieces. A seller might ask for a service instead of coin.
Common items can often be bought in a town or city, and Uncommon and Rare items
usually only in cities. Rarer items might be sold only in wondrous places, such
as a city on another plane of existence.

| Rarity | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Common | 100 GP |
| Uncommon | 400 GP |
| Rare | 4,000 GP |
| Very Rare | 40,000 GP |
| Legendary | 200,000 GP |
| Artifact | Priceless |

- **Consumables.** Halve the value of a consumable item other than a Spell
  Scroll.
- **Equipment.** A magic item that incorporates an item with a purchase cost,
  such as a weapon or a suit of armor, adds that cost to its value. +1 Armor
  (Plate Armor) is worth 5,500 GP: 4,000 GP as a Rare item and 1,500 GP for
  the Plate Armor.

## Spell Scrolls

A Spell Scroll is worth double what it costs to scribe. Scribing takes 8 hours
of work on each day.

| Spell Level | Time to Scribe | Cost to Scribe | Value |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cantrip | 1 day | 15 GP | 30 GP |
| 1 | 1 day | 25 GP | 50 GP |
| 2 | 3 days | 100 GP | 200 GP |
| 3 | 5 days | 150 GP | 300 GP |
| 4 | 10 days | 1,000 GP | 2,000 GP |
| 5 | 25 days | 1,500 GP | 3,000 GP |
| 6 | 40 days | 10,000 GP | 20,000 GP |
| 7 | 50 days | 12,500 GP | 25,000 GP |
| 8 | 60 days | 15,000 GP | 30,000 GP |
| 9 | 120 days | 50,000 GP | 100,000 GP |
