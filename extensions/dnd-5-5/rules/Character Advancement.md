# Character Advancement

Characters earn Experience Points (XP), which the GM awards as they overcome
challenges and complete adventures. When a character's XP total equals or
exceeds a number in the table, the character reaches that level. A character
who starts above level 1 begins with the XP for their starting level: a
character starting at level 10 has 64,000 XP.

| Level | Experience Points | Proficiency Bonus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 0 | +2 |
| 2 | 300 | +2 |
| 3 | 900 | +2 |
| 4 | 2,700 | +2 |
| 5 | 6,500 | +3 |
| 6 | 14,000 | +3 |
| 7 | 23,000 | +3 |
| 8 | 34,000 | +3 |
| 9 | 48,000 | +4 |
| 10 | 64,000 | +4 |
| 11 | 85,000 | +4 |
| 12 | 100,000 | +4 |
| 13 | 120,000 | +5 |
| 14 | 140,000 | +5 |
| 15 | 165,000 | +5 |
| 16 | 195,000 | +5 |
| 17 | 225,000 | +6 |
| 18 | 265,000 | +6 |
| 19 | 305,000 | +6 |
| 20 | 355,000 | +6 |

After level 20, a GM can grant a bonus feat for every 30,000 XP a character
earns above 355,000 XP.

Some campaigns award no XP, and characters gain levels when the GM decides
instead. The table's thresholds then don't apply.
