# Templates

Copy a page below into the appropriate world or campaign folder, rename it, and
fill in its metadata. The built-in Obsidian Templates plugin can insert these
files if you choose to enable it, but no template engine is required.

types use full paths to avoid confusing a template with its type page.
See [[Vault Guide]] for IDs, campaign metadata, and naming rules.
